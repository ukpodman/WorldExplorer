# Citizenship and Heritage Studies: sources and verification

Citizenship and Heritage Studies is a new core senior secondary subject from the 2025/26 session, bringing together Nigerian History, Civic Education and Social Studies (Federal Ministry of Education, 3 September 2025). Published schemes of work disagree about the SSS 1 topic order, so only SSS 1 First Term topics that appear in published schemes are listed; SSS 2 and SSS 3 are left empty with a note until they can be checked.

**World Explorer does not offer examination preparation for this subject** and makes no claim about when or how it will be examined.

### Legal content

The citizenship lesson explains the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999 (as amended) in simple words: s. 24 (duties), ss. 25–28 (citizenship by birth, registration and naturalisation; dual citizenship), ss. 33–46 (fundamental rights and enforcement). It is not legal advice and says so. Civic scenarios are non-partisan: they name no party, politician or current political dispute.

## Verification status of the topic list

Every topic is marked `scheme`: it appears in a published scheme of work that says it follows the revised NERDC curriculum, but it has **not** been checked against the official NERDC curriculum document, which was not accessible to us. The app shows this label on every topic. Before marking any topic `official`, obtain the NERDC document (from NERDC or a State Ministry/SUBEB) and compare topic by topic.
