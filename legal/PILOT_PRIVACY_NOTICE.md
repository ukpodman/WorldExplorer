**Version:** FF10-private-privacy-2026-10-25-v1
**Scope:** Only adult applicants and participants in the invitation-only Friends & Family 10 cash pilot. This notice does not replace or purport to approve the general World Explorer Privacy Policy for the public app.

### The information used
We process the Google sign-in email and associated profile identifier/display name; invitation, application, adult/Nigerian-residence declarations and programme acceptance; learning-event types and timestamps, quizzes, country and challenge interactions; provisional rewards, day reviews, settlement decisions, reasons and audit history. For any real transfer, we also process identity/account-name verification information, bank name and account's final four digits, allocation amount, transfer date, payment reference and relevant financial records. Full bank account details are exchanged separately, not stored in the pilot reward ledger. We do **not** require browser-device fingerprinting, GPS tracking or screen recordings for this private pilot.

### Why we process it and legal basis
We use information to operate this voluntary invitation-only agreement, prevent misuse, calculate and review allocations, make and evidence payments, answer complaints and comply with recordkeeping obligations. Processing needed to perform the participation agreement is considered under **section 25 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023**; processing to comply with a legal obligation may have a separate legal basis. Optional usage analytics require a separate choice and must not be made a condition of the pilot. The organiser remains responsible for its documented lawful-basis assessment.

### Who can see information and international hosting
Only authorised organisers and relevant service providers supporting the app or legitimate compliance needs may access necessary information. **Supabase** stores accounts and pilot records; **Streamlit Community Cloud/Snowflake** hosts the application; **Google** provides sign-in; some country images may come directly from **Wikimedia Commons**. Some processing takes place outside Nigeria. The organiser must check its actual Supabase hosting region and document an appropriate cross-border transfer basis and service-provider arrangements before activating participant-data collection for the pilot. Information is not sold to advertisers.

### Retention and deletion
The organiser should retain pilot applications, activity logs and review decisions only as long as needed to complete the pilot and address disputes, with an intended **12-month review/deletion point after the pilot ends** for non-financial identifiable trial records. Approved payments, settlement records and evidence required for tax/accounting or legal claims are retained for the applicable mandatory period, then securely deleted or anonymised. Before promising an automatic deletion date, the organiser must confirm and operate an actual deletion/retention procedure; the current app may require manual administration to carry this out.

### Rights, choices and complaints
Participants may request access, correction, restriction, deletion (subject to lawful retention), objection and other applicable data rights by writing to **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}**. They can stop taking part without losing rights over allocations already validly approved. Optional analytics can be changed separately in the app's Privacy settings. Complaints may also be made to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission**. Where an application or payment needs identity verification, the organiser may request reasonable information to protect the participant.

### Safety and changes
The organiser should limit access, keep database service keys confidential, secure transport, log owner actions and investigate any data breach under applicable law. A material change to this notice should be communicated to the ten participants and reviewed before the changed processing begins. No wider public cash programme is authorised by this notice.
