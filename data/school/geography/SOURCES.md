## Figures that are illustrative, not data

Worked examples and some questions use **illustrative figures** to practise a method: map scale, lapse rate, dependency ratio, crop yield,
journey time and balance of trade. Each one is labelled as illustrative in the lesson. The Lagos–Ibadan line length (about 156 km) is
approximate.

## Limitations

* The official NERDC curriculum could not be obtained. Class levels follow NERDC-based schemes of work, which differ between schools and
  between the older curriculum and the 2025/26 curriculum.
* Several facts rely on secondary sources such as Wikipedia, which in turn cite primary sources. A teacher review should confirm them against
  current official publications. Census status in particular should be re-checked: the lessons treat the 2006 census as the most recent.
* No teacher has reviewed the content yet. Every lesson shows "Educational review pending".
