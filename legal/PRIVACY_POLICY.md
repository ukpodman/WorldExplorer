## 2. Using World Explorer without an account

You can explore countries, learn and play quizzes **without signing in**. As a guest, your progress is kept only for
your current browser session on our server and is not saved after you leave. We do not ask for your name, email or
location.

## 4. World Explorer — Friends & Family 10

The programme is limited to **at most 10 individually approved adults**. If you apply, we process:

- your Google-verified email address and display name (to identify participants and contact you);
- your declarations that you are 18 or older and live in Nigeria, your Nigerian state of residence, the version of the
  terms you accepted and an optional note you write;
- our decision on your application and any notes we make about it;
- if you are approved: records of your learning interactions (the type of interaction, the country, quiz or question
  concerned, and the time our server recorded it), the provisional rewards calculated from them, the monthly
  settlement and our review decisions, and an audit log of changes. We use them only to run the programme, calculate
  rewards, make and record payments and prevent abuse. We do not use device fingerprinting, location tracking or
  screen recording;
- if you receive cash payments: the account holder's legal name, the bank and the last four digits of the account
  number (the full account number is not stored in the app), and each payment's amount, date and bank transfer
  reference. Tax and accounting law may require us to keep payment records for a set period;
- if a public rewards programme is later approved and you take part: invitation (referral) records and monthly reward
  amounts under that programme's rules.

**Public rewards payments are not active.** Friends & Family 10 cash payments are described above. If public payments are approved in future, a withdrawal request records your full legal name
and your declarations. Bank details are **not** entered in the app; [describe the separate secure channel]. We keep
a one-way, keyed fingerprint of the receiving account (not the account number) to detect duplicate accounts, and the
bank's payment reference. Tax and accounting law may require us to keep payment records for a set period.

## 5. Cookies and similar storage

We use a few small first-party cookies. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

| Name | Purpose | When | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| `we_consent` | Remembers your usage-statistics choice | When you choose | 365 days |
| `we_aid` | Random id for usage statistics | Only after you allow statistics | Up to 400 days; removed when you switch statistics off |
| `we_gpc` | Honours your browser's Global Privacy Control signal | If your browser sends it | While the signal is on |
| `we_handoff` | Finds your guest progress again after Google's sign-in page | Only when you save progress with Google | 30 minutes |
| `we_ref` | Keeps a friend's invitation code through sign-in (pilot) | Only when you open an invitation link | Up to 30 days, removed when used |
| `we_school` | School Learning progress on this device | Only if you switch on on-device progress | Until you switch it off |
| `we_test` | Marks the owner's own test browsers | Owners only | — |
| Sign-in session cookie | Keeps you signed in (set by the hosting platform's sign-in) | When signed in | Until you sign out or it expires |

A browser's Global Privacy Control signal always switches usage statistics off.

- correct it;
- delete it;
- restrict or object to its processing;
- transfer it to you in a portable format.

You can also withdraw consent at any time: switch off usage statistics in Settings → Privacy. Withdrawing consent
does not affect processing that already happened. Contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We may need to confirm that a

## 10. Security

The browser never talks to our database directly. The server uses a private key that never reaches your browser,
and each signed-in user can only read and write their own saved profile. We will notify the NDPC and affected users
of a personal-data breach as the law requires.
