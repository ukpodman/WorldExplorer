## Sources

- Country facts: [mledoze/countries](https://github.com/mledoze/countries), commit `c2ac004` (2026-09-29), 250 entries, licence ODbL 1.0. The adapted database in `data/` is distributed under the same licence.
- Reference list: [UN Member States](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/member-states) (193) and [UN non-member observer states](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/non-member-states) (2), checked 2026-10-08. Stored in `data/reference/un_membership.json` with each UN name mapped to its ISO code.
- Corrections and editorial decisions: `data/reference/curation.json` (each with its reason).
- Facts carried over from the earlier collection: `data/reference/curated_facts.json` (sourced capital roles; UNESCO landmarks, each linked to its UNESCO page).
- Flags: SVG files from the same repository, rasterised to 320 px WebP. Flags are **not** covered by the ODbL; most national flags are in the public domain, but some contain emblems with separate rights or legal restrictions (see [Wikipedia: Copyright on emblems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Copyright_on_emblems)).
- Heads of state: `data/heads_of_state.json`, each with source and `verified_on` date (not advanced without rechecking).

## Excluded upstream entries

- `ATA` — Antarctica is a continent governed by the Antarctic Treaty, not a country or territory with a capital.
- `ATF` — Mostly uninhabited Antarctic and sub-Antarctic lands with no permanent population or capital.
- `BVT` — Uninhabited sub-Antarctic island.
- `HMD` — Uninhabited sub-Antarctic islands.
- `SGS` — No permanent population; sub-Antarctic.
- `UMI` — Scattered, essentially uninhabited islands in the Pacific and Caribbean; no capital and no single continent.

## Facts that skip a question type

Missing or disputed data removes only the affected question type; the place still appears everywhere else.

- Capital questions skipped for `ISR`: The status of Jerusalem as capital is disputed internationally.
- Capital questions skipped for `PSE`: The capital is disputed; Ramallah is the administrative seat.
- Capital questions skipped for `ESH`: Sovereignty is disputed.
- Capital questions skipped for `NRU`: Nauru has no official capital; Yaren is the seat of government.
- Capital questions skipped for `BES`: Each island has its own administrative centre.
- Currency questions skipped for `ZWE`: Multi-currency system with recent changes; the upstream list mixes legal tenders and is not reliable enough to quiz.
- Currency questions skipped for `ESH`: Currency use differs across the disputed territory.
- Continent questions skipped for transcontinental or differently classified places: RUS, TUR, KAZ, AZE, GEO, ARM, CYP, EGY, IDN. They appear under each listed continent in filters.
- Places without currency data: FSM.
- Places without a listed capital: MAC.
- Calling-code questions only where one place alone uses the code (196 places). Shared codes such as +1 (North America) and +7 (Russia and Kazakhstan) are never asked.
- Internet-domain questions only where one place alone uses the domain (240 places).
- UNESCO landmark questions: 71 countries have single-country sites carried over from the earlier collection, each linked to its UNESCO page. Other countries have no landmark questions yet (the UNESCO data service was not reachable from the build environment).
