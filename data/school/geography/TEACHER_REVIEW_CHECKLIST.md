## C. Items to check first (known uncertainties)

* Class levels for each lesson. They come from NERDC-based schemes of work, because the official NERDC document was not available.
* Census status and population figures after 2006 (labelled as estimates).
* Rainfall and temperature figures (rounded climate summaries).
* Vegetation belt boundaries on the map, which are drawn as simplified bands.
* The salinisation and laterisation wording in Lesson 3.
* Recent infrastructure: railway lines, Lekki port and the Dangote refinery.
* Trade statistics (NBS Q4 2024, quoted with its date).
